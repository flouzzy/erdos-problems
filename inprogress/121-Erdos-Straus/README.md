# Erdős-Straus Conjecture (Problem 121)

## Status: In Progress

## Formal Statement
The Erdős-Straus conjecture posits that for any integer $n \geq 2$, the rational number $4/n$ can be expressed as the sum of three unit fractions. Formally, there exist strictly positive integers $x, y, z \in \mathbb{Z}^+$ such that:

$$ \frac{4}{n} = \frac{1}{x} + \frac{1}{y} + \frac{1}{z} $$

## Current Research Approach
Our current research involves structurally analyzing congruence classes modulo $4$ and proposing constructive proofs for these subsets.
