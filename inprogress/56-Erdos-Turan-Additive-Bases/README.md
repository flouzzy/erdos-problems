# Erdős-Turán Conjecture on Additive Bases

This directory contains research on the Erdős-Turán Conjecture on Additive Bases.
The conjecture states that if an asymptotic basis of order 2 exists for the natural numbers, then the number of representations of $n$ as the sum of two elements in the basis cannot be bounded.

## Current Progress

- The formal proof generation and autoformalization architecture are in progress.
- Zero-ellipse proof strategy and probabilistic approaches are explored.
